About Dubai

Dubai is one of the most spectacular cities in the world, located on the southeastern coast of the Persian Gulf in the United Arab Emirates. Known as the "City of Gold," Dubai dazzles visitors with its futuristic skyline, world-record landmarks, pristine beaches, and legendary hospitality. Whether you are seeking luxury shopping, thrilling desert adventures, or world-class cuisine, Dubai has something extraordinary for every traveller.

From the iconic Burj Khalifa the tallest building in the world to the vast golden sand dunes of the Arabian Desert, Dubai offers an unforgettable experience that blends ancient Arabian culture with ultra-modern innovation. It is a city that never stops surprising you.

🌟 Best Time to Visit: November to March when the weather is cool and pleasant, perfect for outdoor activities and sightseeing.

Top Things to See

Dubai is packed with world-class attractions that will leave you breathless. Here are the must-see highlights:

  • 🏙️ Burj Khalifa — Visit the observation deck on the 148th floor for stunning panoramic views of the city
  • 🛍️ Dubai Mall — One of the world's largest shopping malls with over 1,200 stores, an aquarium, and an ice rink
  • 🏖️ Jumeirah Beach — Relax on the beautiful white sand beaches with views of the iconic Burj Al Arab hotel
  • 🎡 Dubai Frame — A giant picture frame structure offering views of old and new Dubai simultaneously
  • 🕌 Al Fahidi Historical Neighbourhood — Explore the old city with traditional wind towers and cultural museums
  • 🌊 Palm Jumeirah — The world-famous palm-shaped artificial island lined with luxury hotels and residences
  • 🎆 Dubai Fountain — The world's largest choreographed fountain system performing dazzling shows every evening

Places to Go

Beyond the iconic landmarks, Dubai offers incredible experiences for every type of traveller:

  1. Desert Safari — Experience dune bashing, camel riding, and traditional Bedouin dinner under the stars
  2. Dubai Creek — Take an Abra (traditional wooden boat) ride across the historic creek to explore the gold and spice souks
  3. Miracle Garden — Walk through 150 million flowers arranged in breathtaking designs — the world's largest natural flower garden
  4. Global Village — Explore pavilions from over 90 countries showcasing food, culture, and entertainment
  5. Atlantis Aquaventure Waterpark — One of the world's best waterparks on Palm Jumeirah with thrilling rides for all ages
  6. Dubai Opera — Enjoy world-class performances in a stunning dhow-shaped opera house in Downtown Dubai

Travel Tips

✅ Visa: Most nationalities can get a visa on arrival or apply online before travelling.

✅ Currency: UAE Dirham (AED). Most places accept credit cards.

✅ Language: Arabic is the official language, but English is widely spoken everywhere.

✅ Dress Code: Dress modestly in public areas and religious sites. Swimwear is fine at beaches and pools.

✅ Transport: Dubai has an excellent metro system, taxis, and ride-hailing apps like Uber and Careem.

✅ Safety: Dubai is one of the safest cities in the world with very low crime rates.
